# 5.1. Logging · Ghi chép
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Tab of 5.0 (default tab) |
| Status | Designed (two states) |

## Purpose
See and log the period's spending and income.

## Entry & exits
- The default tab when 5.0 opens, or the "Ghi chép" tab in the bottom bar.
- Tap an expense → 5.4 (edit mode). Tap an income → 5.9 (edit mode). Tap a savings row → 5.10 (edit mode).
- ＋ → 5.4 (toggle on Chi tiêu) or 5.9 (toggle on Thu nhập) (R5).
- "Gửi tiết kiệm" button (toggle on Thu nhập) → 5.10 with "Gửi vào" selected (D51).

## Layout (below the shared 5.0 header and summary card)
1. **Toggle** (segmented control): **"Chi tiêu (N)"** | **"Thu nhập (N)"**, where N is the number of entries (savings transfers count under Thu nhập).
1b. **Savings button** (toggle on Thu nhập only, D51), full width right under the toggle: yellow gradient card (`#FDE047` → `#EAB308`), a white-tinted 🐷 tile, the title **"Gửi tiết kiệm"** (bold, dark brown) with the small value line **"Quỹ <fund balance>"** below it (R6.2), and a dark-brown round **"＋"** on the right. It scrolls with the list.
2. **Day boxes**, newest day first. Each box (light grey `#F1F5F9`, rounded) has:
   - a header with **only the date**, "Thứ 4 · 30/09" (D40);
   - entry rows, separated by 4px gaps.
3. **Expense row** (toggle on Chi tiêu, D42), one compact line:
   - description · **category tag** (a small pill in the category's dark color) · amount with a **"−" sign** in dark text, e.g. "−60" (D49);
   - the **row background is the category's pastel color** (palette in `visual-style.md`);
   - no icon and no subtext;
   - **large expense:** the amount is **red** (`#E11D48`) when it is at or above the 6.0 threshold "Tô màu khoản chi lớn từ" (R3.4). Sample: 318 and 250 with the threshold 200.
4. **Untracked row:** white background, dashed border, "Không danh mục" in grey italics, the amount with "−", and no tag. Large untracked amounts are red too (R3.4).
5. **Income row** (toggle on Thu nhập): green background, description, amount in green with "+", and no tag. A negative carry-over shows "−" (D60).
6. **Savings row** (toggle on Thu nhập, D50): amber background (`#FEF9C3`), the fixed text **"Gửi tiết kiệm"** with "−<amount>" or **"Rút tiết kiệm"** with "+<amount>", amount in dark amber, no tag. Sorted with incomes by R3.3.

## States & variants
- **Chi tiêu** (`#s5-1`) / **Thu nhập** (`#s5-1b`).
- **Empty list:** no day boxes, just the ＋ button. No message (D25).

## Rules
- Day grouping and order: **R3.3**. Untracked entries: **R3.1**. Large-expense highlight: **R3.4**. ＋: **R5**. Savings rows: **R6.3**.
