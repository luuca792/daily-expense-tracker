## Purpose
Everything about one period. This spec covers the parts shared by all tabs; each tab has its own spec.

## Entry & exits
- From 4.0 (tap a period) or after creating a period in 4.1. It opens on the 5.1 tab.
- Back "‹" → 4.0. ⚙ → 6.0.
- ⋯ → menu: **"Sửa kỳ"** (→ 4.1 edit mode) · **"Xóa kỳ"** (confirmation + undo, D61).

## Layout, shared (top → bottom)
1. **Header:**
   - "‹" back button.
   - Period name (bold), with the dates on a small teal line below it: "dd/MM → dd/MM/yyyy" (R1.4).
   - On the right, two round icon buttons: ⋯ and ⚙.
2. **Summary card** (teal gradient), on every tab:
   - Left: label "Còn lại", a **large** amount (Còn lại), and a small line below it, "Số dư <amount>". The reserve is not shown (D37).
   - Right: "Thu +<amount>" and "Chi −<amount>". Thu includes savings transfers (R6.3).
3. **Tab content** (5.1 / 5.2 / 5.3).
4. **Bottom bar** (white, top border, about 62px), three tabs with an icon and a label:
   - 📝 **"Ghi chép"** (5.1) · 🎯 **"Mục tiêu"** (5.2) · 📊 **"Thống kê"** (5.3).
   - Active tab: teal label and a light-teal pill behind the icon. Inactive tabs: grey.
5. **Floating ＋ button** (teal gradient circle) above the bottom bar on the right, shown on 5.1 and 5.2 only (R5).

## Interactions
- The ＋ action depends on the tab and the toggle: **R5**.
- Switching tabs keeps the period; the 5.1 toggle state is remembered within the period (R5).

## Rules
- Còn lại / Số dư / reserve: **R4** (D35–D37). Savings transfers in Thu: **R6.3**.
