## Purpose
Add or edit an income entry, including editing or deleting the carry-over entry "Còn lại từ tháng trước" (R1.8).

## Entry & exits
- ＋ on 5.1 with the toggle on Thu nhập (R5), or tap an income row (edit mode). Savings transfers use 5.10, not this sheet (D50, D51).

## Proposed layout (mirrors 5.4, with no category grid)
1. Title **"Thêm thu nhập"** / **"Sửa thu nhập"** · ✕.
2. Amount box "Số tiền" (green accent). The amount may be negative, so a negative carry-over (D60) can be edited.
3. **"Mô tả"**: text input.
4. **"Ngày"**: date picker (default as in R3.2), inside the period only (R3.5).
5. **"Lưu & thêm tiếp"** · **"Lưu"**. Edit mode adds a destructive **"Xóa"**.

Income types (salary, borrowed…) are not included for now (L-09).
