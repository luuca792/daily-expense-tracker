## Purpose
Charts for the period, one chart per page. The user swipes between them.

## Entry & exits
- The "Thống kê" tab in the bottom bar. There is **no ＋** on this tab (R5).
- Opens on chart 1.

## Layout (below the shared 5.0 header and summary card)
A **chart carousel** (D46):
- Each page shows one ring chart with the **chart name** centered under it (bold), followed by that chart's own details.
- **Arrows** in small round buttons at the left (‹) and right (›) edges of the chart, vertically centered. Page 1 has no ‹ and the last page has no ›. Tapping an arrow or swiping left/right changes the page.
- There are no page dots and no section heading above the chart.

### Chart 1 · "Danh mục & không danh mục" (`#s5-3`)
1. **Ring chart:** Sinh hoạt spent / maximum. Categorized Sinh hoạt spending is solid orange, untracked spending is a light-orange segment, and the rest is grey.
   - Center text: "Đã chi", **<spent>**, "/ <maximum> · <pct>%".
2. Chart name **"Danh mục & không danh mục"**.
3. **Value row** (green): "Còn lại" ... <maximum − spent>. It is red when negative (R4).
4. Label **"Chi sinh hoạt theo ngày"**, followed by a **daily bar chart** (one bar per day of the period) with axis labels for the first, middle and last day.

The two legend cards ("Có danh mục" / "Không danh mục" with amount and count) were removed, since the chart name already says what the ring shows (D46). If a legend comes back, the label is **"Có danh mục"** (not "Có danh mục Sinh hoạt").

### Chart 2 · "Mục tiêu" (`#s5-3b`)
1. **Ring chart:** the share of each goal in the **total spent** of the period (Chi, e.g. 9.500), not in its target. One segment per goal, including Sinh hoạt (which includes untracked spending). Each segment uses **the goal's own color**, the same one as its icon tile on 5.2 (set by the user on 5.6).
   - Center text: "Đã chi", **<total spent>**.
2. Chart name **"Mục tiêu"**.
3. **Legend**, two columns: color dot · goal name · spent amount.

Segment and legend order: R4.1.

## States & variants
- Nothing spent yet: the rings are empty (grey), with "Đã chi 0".

## Rules
- Sinh hoạt spent and remaining: **R4**. Untracked spending always counts toward Sinh hoạt: **R3.1**, **R2.2**. Chart 2 shares and order: **R4.1**.
