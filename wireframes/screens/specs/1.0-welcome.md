## Purpose
The first screen of a fresh install: start with empty data.

## Entry & exits

1. **Gradient header** (teal → violet, rounded bottom corners) containing the logo tile "₫" and the title "Sổ chi tiêu".
2. Empty space.
3. Primary button **"Bắt đầu mới"**.

No tagline and no privacy note (D25).
