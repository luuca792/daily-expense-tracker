## Purpose
App-wide options, plus the expected Sinh hoạt maximum used when a new period has no previous period to copy from (no periods yet, or "Không chọn") (D62).

## Entry & exits
- ⚙ on 2.0 or 5.0. Back "‹" → the previous screen.
- Each "value ›" row opens a small edit sheet for that value.

## Layout (top → bottom)
Sections, each with a small teal uppercase heading and a white card of rows:
1. **"🍜 SINH HOẠT"**
   - "Mức sinh hoạt tối đa mong đợi" → value "4.000 ›"
2. **"⚠️ CẢNH BÁO"**
   - "Tô màu khoản chi lớn từ" → "200 ›" (one number: the minimum amount that is highlighted in red on 5.1, R3.4)

Rows have no subtitles (D25).

Removed (D48): the "Tính khoản không danh mục vào Sinh hoạt" toggle (untracked always counts, Sinh hoạt is the default goal), the "Mục tiêu" section (starting goals), the "Hiển thị" section (amounts are always in thousands of đồng), "Khôi phục từ tệp" and "Xóa dữ liệu trên máy này". Removed (D56): the whole "Dữ liệu" section ("Sao lưu (tải xuống)"); data stays on the device only.

## Rules
- The expected maximum is used only when no previous period is selected; it never changes existing periods: **R1.6**. Large-expense highlight: **R3.4**.
