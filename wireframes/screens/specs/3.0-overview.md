## Purpose
Answer "how much do I have in total?": the savings fund plus the money left in the **active period**, with each part shown clearly.

## Entry & exits
- From 2.0 → "Tổng quan". Back "‹" → 2.0.
- Savings card → 7.0.
- Active period card → 5.0 of that period (opens on 5.1).

## Layout (top → bottom)
1. Header: "‹" and the title **"Tổng quan"**.
2. **Total card** (violet gradient, like the 2.0 hub card):
   - label **"Tổng tài sản"**;
   - the total, very large (R7.2);
   - a **two-part bar** below it: amber = savings, teal = active period, each segment sized by its share of the total. Hidden when either part is ≤ 0 (R7.2).
3. Label **"Tiết kiệm"**, then the **savings card** (white, amber left edge): 🐷 tile, **"Quỹ tiết kiệm"**, a small line with its share ("37%"), the fund balance (R6.2) on the right, "›".
4. Label **"Kỳ đang theo dõi"**, then the **period card** (white, teal left edge): 🗓 tile, the period name, the dates line as on 4.0 (R1.4), a small line **"Số dư · <share>"**, the period's **Số dư** on the right, "›".

No reserve, Còn lại or goal numbers on this page (D54).

## States & variants
- **No periods yet:** the "Kỳ đang theo dõi" label and card are hidden; the total = the fund; no bar.
- **No savings ever** (fund 0, no base): the savings card still shows 0, so the user sees where savings would go.
- **A negative part** (Số dư < 0, or the fund < 0 after deleting the base, R6.7): the amount shows "−", the shares and the bar are hidden, and the total still adds both parts.

## Rules
- Active period: **R7.1**. Total and shares: **R7.2**. Fund balance: **R6.2**. Số dư: **R4**.
