## Purpose
See the savings fund and where every amount came from or went to: which period put money in, which period took money out.

## Entry & exits
- "Tiết kiệm" hub card on 2.0.
- Back "‹" → 2.0.
- Tap a transfer row → 5.0 of that row's period, opened on the **5.1 tab with the toggle on Thu nhập**, where the transfer row is (R6.5).
- "Số dư ban đầu" button (only while there is no base savings) → 7.1 in create mode. The base savings row → 7.1 in edit mode (D52, R6.7).
- Period transfers are made only inside a period, through 5.10 (R6.1).

## Layout (top → bottom)
1. Header: "‹" and the title **"Tiết kiệm"**.
2. **Fund card** (amber gradient, same shape as the 5.0 summary card):
   - Left: label **"Quỹ tiết kiệm"** and the fund balance, large (R6.2).
   - Right: **"Gửi +<total in>"** and **"Rút −<total out>"**, all time.
3. **Transfer list**, newest first (R6.2). One card per transfer (same card style as 4.0):
   - an icon tile on amber: 📥 for "Gửi vào", 📤 for "Rút ra";
   - **period name** (bold);
   - a small line: **"dd/MM/yyyy · Gửi vào"** or **"… · Rút ra"**;
   - the amount from the fund's point of view: **"+3.000"** in green for Gửi vào, **"−2.000"** in dark text for Rút ra;
   - "›".
4. **Base savings row** (D52), always **last** in the list: light amber card, 🏦 tile, the label **"Số dư ban đầu"** (no period, no date line), the amount "+1.000" in green, **no "›"** (it links to no period). An empty space the width of "›" keeps its amount aligned with the other rows (D53).
5. **"Số dư ban đầu" button** (only while there is no base savings): full-width dashed amber card with a 🏦 tile, the label **"Số dư ban đầu"** and an amber round "＋". It sits right under the fund card.

## States & variants
- **Empty / new user** (`#s7-0b`): the fund card shows 0, "Gửi +0", "Rút −0", then only the "Số dư ban đầu" button. No message (D25).
- **Fund below 0** (only possible after deleting the base savings, R6.7): the balance is shown with "−" and withdrawals are blocked until it is back above 0.

## Rules
- Fund balance, list order: **R6.2**. Navigation to the period: **R6.5**. Base savings: **R6.7**.
