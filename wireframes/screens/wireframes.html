0c · Tiết kiệm (new, D50, for review)

One savings fund that starts at 0. Money moves in or out only from inside a period (the yellow "Gửi tiết kiệm" button on 5.1 Thu nhập, see 5.1): Gửi vào takes it from the period's income, Rút ra adds it to the period's income. 7.0 shows the fund and every transfer with its period; tapping one opens that period's Ghi chép tab. Rules: R6.
